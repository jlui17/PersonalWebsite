One card per section, in a column on the right. A card holds three things: the plain title, one line that says what is inside in other words than the open section uses, and the invite with a chevron. The selected card takes the open pane's amber border and a filled dot, and loses its invite. On hover the border steps up to the strong line and the invite turns amber. All six always show, so nothing in the column moves when a visitor selects a step.
