The pathway is the strip along the top of the window where his sprite walks, under the title bar, on the page's own ground. It has one step per section. From its floor hang the steps' tabs, one under each step, each with its number and a few plain words. Selecting a step, by a key, a click or the search, opens its section in the pane under the tabs and sends him walking to it. The selected step's tab is amber with a filled dot, and it joins the pane's top border. Each step has its residents at home all the time: the shoe rack, the desk with the two robots beside it, Truffle asleep, the coffee corner with the ball beside it, and the cow. Here step 1 is selected and he holds two shoeboxes from the rack. The floor is the top of the tabs, and every sprite stands on it at true size. Where a tab is too narrow for its words on one line (640 to 1050px), they take two and the dot goes. On a phone the pathway shows the selected step alone, and the row of tabs scrolls sideways.
