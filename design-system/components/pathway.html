The pathway is the strip along the bottom where his sprite walks. It has one step per section, in the order of the cards, and under it the step bar names each step. Selecting a step, by a key, a click or the search, opens its section in the pane and sends him walking to it. The selected step is amber with a bar under it. Each step has its residents at home all the time: the shoe rack, Truffle asleep, the two robots, the cow, the coffee corner with the ball beside it, and the desk. Here step 1 is selected and he holds two shoeboxes from the rack. The floor is the step bar's top rule, and every sprite stands on it at true size. On a phone the pathway shows the selected step alone.
