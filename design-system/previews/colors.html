One dark palette. The ground is a dark roast coffee brown, never black, and every surface and hairline is that brown mixed a little towards cream. There are two accents: amber for focus and attention, blue for links. Meaning carried by colour uses blue against amber, never red against green, and it is always backed by a label or a shape.
