The site is one window that never scrolls as a whole. The open pane's body scrolls, and so does the column of cards when it has to. Content flows from the top with one even rhythm. Nothing is stretched, spread or pinned to the bottom: room left at the end of a pane stays empty.

The window

title bar, --pn-title-h 40px
the pane: the rest of the width. 10px from the window's edge and from the cards. Body padding 24px 26px. cards, --pn-stack-w 424px, at most half4px apart, padding --pn-fold-pad
pathway, --pn-pathway-h 68px: the tallest pose (62px) plus a little air
step bar, --pn-bar-h 30px

Corners are --pn-radius, 6px, on the pane, the cards and the sign. Buttons inside them take 4px. Every border and rule is 1px. There are no drop shadows on the page. The search box, which floats over it, is the one exception.

The vertical rhythm inside a pane

--pn-gap-above-rule

26px, up to 42px. From the end of a part to the hairline that opens the next part.

--pn-gap-below-rule

22px, up to 38px. From that hairline to the part's first line.

--pn-gap-hook

18px, up to 34px. From the hook, or the quieter line under it, to the first part.

--pn-gap-block

18px, up to 34px. From block to block, in every column.

--pn-line-pad

5px, up to 7px. Over and under each line of a list.

--pn-gap-heading

4px, up to 8px. From a "###" to its text.

The first number is the step in a 1280x800 window. In a taller pane all the steps grow together, by one rule (--pn-rise in tokens.css), up to the second number. The ranks keep their order, and no step between two things passes 48px. The card for the pane shows the rhythm in use. Columns inside a pane are 32px apart. Paragraphs keep a 62ch measure, so a wider pane gives its room to the columns and the pictures, never to longer lines.
