Two families, split by reading level. JetBrains Mono is everything a visitor sees when they skim: titles, hooks, headings, labels, buttons, the cards, the step bar. Instrument Sans is the details: the sentences under a heading. There is no third family, not even for an arrow or a key: those are drawn. The ranks step down by size, weight and colour.
