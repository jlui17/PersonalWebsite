One latte palette, no dark mode. The page is deliberately dimmer than white; the ink is espresso, not black; the card fills are the page colour pushed one and two steps deeper. Copper is the only accent. Meaning never rides on colour alone: every coloured element also carries a label or a shape.

  • Background--ss-bg #f3e8d8

    The page. Also the speech bubble fill.

  • Tint--ss-tint #ecdec7

    Every card fill: status readout, people, the aside.

  • Tint, deep--ss-tint-deep #e7d5b8

    The girlfriend card only. The one deliberate highlight.

  • Ink--ss-ink #33291f

    Headings, values, links, the masthead name.

  • Muted--ss-muted #6f6357

    Labels, body copy under a heading, the clock.

  • Accent--ss-accent #a5642f

    Copper. Section numbers, the STATUS word, project tags, link hover.

  • Sprite ink--ss-sprite-ink #1e1711

    Outline of every pixel sprite. Not for text.

  • Glow--ss-glow rgba(196,132,72,.14)

    A faint warm radial at the page's top-left corner.
